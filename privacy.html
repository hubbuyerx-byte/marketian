Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 19, 2026

Marketian (marketian.store), operated by Farman Ali, is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what personal data we collect, how we use it, and your rights regarding your information.

1. Information We Collect

We collect the following personal information when you fill out our checkout form:

Data Field Purpose
Full Name To identify you and personalise your course access
WhatsApp Number To verify payment, deliver course access, and provide support
City For demographic analytics and to improve our marketing

We do not collect sensitive financial information such as bank account numbers, EasyPaisa PINs, or credit card details through our website.

2. How We Use Your Information

Your personal data is used for the following purposes:

  • Payment verification — to confirm your payment via WhatsApp and grant course access.
  • Course delivery — to provide you with the purchased digital product and support.
  • Communication — to send order updates, course access links, and respond to your queries via WhatsApp.
  • Analytics — to understand our audience demographics (aggregate, non-identifying data).

3. Meta Pixel & Tracking

Our website uses the Meta (Facebook) Pixel (ID: 1622955485439618) to:

  • Track page views and website activity for advertising performance measurement.
  • Create custom audiences for Meta Ads retargeting campaigns.
  • Optimise our ad delivery to reach people likely to be interested in our courses.
  • Track conversion events (e.g., checkout initiation, lead submission).

The Meta Pixel uses cookies and similar technologies. You can learn more and manage your Meta ad preferences at facebook.com/adpreferences.

4. Local Storage (localStorage)

We use your browser's localStorage to:

  • Save your checkout form progress so you don't lose your data if you navigate away.
  • Store session-related data to improve your user experience on our website.
  • Remember your checkout step and form state.

localStorage data is stored only on your device and is not transmitted to our servers automatically. You can clear this data at any time through your browser settings.

5. Google Sheets Webhook

When you submit your checkout form, your data (name, WhatsApp number, city) is sent to a Google Sheets webhook for:

  • Recording lead and customer information for order management.
  • Tracking enrollment numbers and payment verification status.
  • Internal business operations and customer support follow-ups.

This data is stored in a private, access-controlled Google Sheet and is only accessible by the Marketian team.

6. WhatsApp Communication

By providing your WhatsApp number during checkout, you consent to receive messages from Marketian via WhatsApp for:

  • Payment verification and order confirmation.
  • Course access link delivery.
  • Customer support and query resolution.
  • Occasional updates about new courses or offers (you can opt out at any time by messaging "STOP").

7. Data Sharing & Third Parties

We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal information to third parties.

We may share limited data with the following service providers solely to operate our business:

  • Meta (Facebook) — anonymised tracking data via the Meta Pixel for ad optimisation.
  • Google — checkout form data via Google Sheets webhook for order management.
  • Vercel — our website hosting provider, which may process server logs containing IP addresses.

These third parties are bound by their own privacy policies and are not permitted to use your data for purposes other than providing services to Marketian.

8. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect your personal information, including:

  • Using HTTPS (SSL encryption) across our entire website.
  • Restricting access to personal data to authorised team members only.
  • Using access-controlled Google Sheets for data storage.

However, no method of transmission over the Internet is 100% secure. While we strive to protect your data, we cannot guarantee absolute security.

9. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access your personal data — request a copy of the information we hold about you.
  • Correct inaccurate data — ask us to update your name, WhatsApp number, or city.
  • Delete your data — request that we remove your personal information from our records.
  • Opt out of WhatsApp marketing messages at any time.

To exercise any of these rights, contact us via WhatsApp at +92 325 509 0258.

10. Children's Privacy

Marketian does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 16 years of age. If we discover that we have collected data from a child under 16, we will delete it promptly.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date. We encourage you to review this policy periodically.
